4. Password Vault

The Password Vault lets you store usernames, passwords, and other sensitive credentials securely on your device. It is protected by a master credential that only you know.

4.1 How You Lock the Vault

When setting up the Password Vault, you choose one of the following protection methods:

  • Master PIN — a numeric PIN you create
  • Master Password — a text password you create

You also set up security questions and answers during setup. These are the only way to recover access if you forget your master PIN or password.

⚠️ Critical Warning — Please Read This

Your Password Vault is stored only on your device. We cannot access it, reset it, or recover it for you.

  • If you forget your master PIN or password, you can recover access by correctly answering the security questions you set during setup.
  • If you forget your master PIN or password AND cannot answer your security questions correctly, all your password vault data will be permanently and irreversibly deleted. There is no way to get it back.
  • We cannot recover your vault data. The encryption key is tied to your credentials and stored locally — not on any server.
  • We strongly recommend writing down your master PIN or password and your security question answers somewhere safe — such as a physical notebook or a separate password manager.
  • We are not responsible for any loss of vault data due to a forgotten PIN, forgotten password, or incorrect security answers.

All vault data is encrypted with AES-256 and stored in an encrypted local database on your device. Even we — the people who built MonoNote — cannot see the contents of your vault.

5. Safe Box (Media Vault)

Safe Box lets you import photos and videos into a private, hidden folder inside MonoNote. Once imported, they are no longer visible in your device gallery or file manager.

⚠️ Critical Warning — Please Read This

What happens when you import media into Safe Box:

  • Your media file is copied into a private folder inside MonoNote's storage.
  • A hidden .nomedia file is placed in that folder so gallery and file manager apps cannot see it.
  • The original file is permanently deleted from your device's public storage — including your gallery, DCIM folder, and Downloads. This is intentional and done to protect your privacy.
  • Once deleted from public storage, the original file cannot be recovered from the gallery or any file manager.

If you forget your vault master PIN or password and cannot answer your security questions,you may lose all Safe Box media files and will be permanently and irreversibly lost.

We strongly recommend backing up your Safe Box media regularly using the Backup feature in the app (Settings → Backup & Restore or Auto Backup for Premium users).

We are not responsible for any loss of Safe Box media due to forgotten PIN or password, vault reset, device failure, or failure to back up.

6. Trash Retention

When you delete notes, password entries, to-do lists, or Safe Box media, they go to the trash instead of being immediately deleted. How long they stay in trash depends on your plan:

  • Free users: Trashed items are permanently deleted after 7 days.
  • Premium users: Trashed items are permanently deleted after 30 days.

You can restore items from trash at any time during the retention period. After the period expires, deletion is permanent and irreversible. You can also manually empty the trash at any time, which permanently deletes all trashed items immediately.

7. Cloud Sync (Premium)

Cloud Sync is available to Premium subscribers only. Here is how it works:

  • What is synced: Notes and password vault entries. Passwords are encrypted on your device before being sent to the server — we never see plaintext passwords.
  • Conflict resolution: If the same note is edited on two devices before syncing, the version with the most recent save time wins. This is called last-write-wins.
  • Real-time: Changes push to other devices within seconds, with a 5-second delay to avoid excessive updates.
  • Rate limiting: Sync runs at most once every 30 seconds per device.
  • Turning it off: You can disable sync in Settings at any time. This stops future syncing but does not delete already synced data from the server.
