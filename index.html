4. Password Vault

The Password Vault lets you store usernames, passwords, and other sensitive information securely on your device.

⚠️ Critical Warning — Read Carefully

Your password vault data is stored locally on your device only. It is protected by a master PIN that you set during setup. Here is what you need to know:

  • If you forget your master PIN, you can recover access by correctly answering the three security questions you set up during vault configuration.
  • If you forget your master PIN AND cannot correctly answer your security questions, all your password vault data will be permanently and irreversibly lost.
  • We cannot recover your vault data for you. The encryption keys are stored locally on your device and are inaccessible without your PIN or correct security answers.
  • We strongly recommend writing down your master PIN and your security question answers in a safe physical location or a separate password manager.
  • We are not liable for any loss of vault data resulting from a forgotten PIN or incorrect security answers.

Your vault data is encrypted with AES-256 and stored in an encrypted Hive database on your device. Even we — the developers — cannot access your vault contents without your PIN.

5. Safe Box (Media Vault)

The Safe Box feature allows you to import photos and videos into a private, encrypted folder within the app.

⚠️ Critical Warning — Read Carefully

When you import media into the Safe Box, the following happens:

  • The original file is copied to a private app directory within your device storage.
  • A .nomedia file is placed in the directory to prevent gallery and file manager apps from scanning it.
  • The original file is permanently deleted from your device's public storage (gallery, DCIM folder, etc.) using multiple secure deletion methods. This is done to protect your privacy.
  • Once deleted from public storage, the original media cannot be recovered from the gallery or file manager.

If your password vault PIN is forgotten and the vault is reset, all Safe Box media will be permanently and irreversibly lost.

We strongly recommend backing up your Safe Box media to Google Drive regularly using the manual or auto-backup features available in the app.

We are not liable for any loss of Safe Box media due to forgotten PIN, vault reset, device failure, or failure to back up.

6. Trash Retention Policy

When you delete notes, password entries, or Safe Box media, they are moved to a trash folder instead of being permanently deleted immediately. The trash retention period depends on your account type:

  • Free users: Items in trash are automatically and permanently purged after 7 days.
  • Premium users: Items in trash are automatically and permanently purged after 30 days.

During the retention period, you can restore trashed items. After the retention period expires, items are permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.

You also have the option to manually empty all trash at any time from the trash screen. This will permanently delete all trashed items immediately, regardless of the retention period.

7. Cloud Sync

Cloud Sync is a premium feature that allows you to synchronize your notes and password entries across multiple devices:

  • Eligibility: Only available to active Premium subscribers.
  • Data synced: Notes and password vault entries. Passwords are encrypted end-to-end on your device before being sent to the server.
  • Conflict resolution: Sync uses a last-write-wins strategy based on the updated_at timestamp. If a conflict occurs, the version with the most recent timestamp wins. On first sync, the remote (server) version takes precedence.
  • Rate limiting: Sync is rate-limited to once every 30 seconds to prevent excessive network usage.
  • Real-time sync: Changes on one device are pushed to other devices in near real-time, with a 5-second debounce interval.
  • Disabling: You can disable Cloud Sync at any time from the Settings screen. Disabling sync stops future syncing but does not delete already synced data from the server.

8. Google Drive Backup

MonoNote can back up your data to a hidden private folder in your own Google Drive account (the drive.appdata folder). This folder is not visible in your Google Drive interface and is only accessible by MonoNote. The following data can be backed up:

  • Notes — backed up as JSON files with version history (up to 3 previous versions preserved).
  • To-do lists — backed up as JSON files with version history.
  • Password vault entries — backed up as encrypted JSON files.
  • Safe Box media — photos and videos uploaded as content-hash-named files.

Backup can be performed manually or automatically (auto-backup is a premium feature). We do not have access to your Google Drive files — they are stored in your own account.
